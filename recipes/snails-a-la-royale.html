❧ Snails a la royale

This is the receipt as done by my great great great grandmother.

Ingredients

  • Pre-cooked canned snails from the supermarket
  • Empty snail shells

Steps

  1. Warm up the snails in the microwave
  2. Stamp the shells with your favourite monarchy colours. Place the snails carefully in the shells with tweezers.

Eating these will make you feel regal.
