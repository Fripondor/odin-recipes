Unhealthy Cake

A tempting cake to be eaten with moderation.

Ingredients

  • 1x small tyre
  • 2x mini mini flags
  • 1x rim adapted to the tyre

Steps

  1. Clean the tyre, dress it on the plate
  2. Plant the flags in the tyre
  3. Install the rim and pump air until it looks plumpy
  4. Serve cold
  5. Eat with your eyes only
